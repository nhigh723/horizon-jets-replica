On-Demand Charter

Whether you’re heading to a business meeting in New York or a weekend escape in Aspen, Horizon Jets makes private air travel effortless. With our On-Demand Charter service, you have full control — simply request your flight, confirm the details, and you’re cleared for takeoff.

Book in Minutes. Fly in Luxury.

  • Submit your flight request online or through your dedicated advisor.

  • Choose from a curated selection of aircraft tailored to your route, passenger count, and preferences.

  • Confirm your itinerary and receive a detailed booking email including:

    • Departure and arrival times

    • FBO (private terminal) locations

    • Aircraft details

    • Pilot information (as applicable)

    • Contact number for your dedicated Aviation Advisor

  • Your Horizon Jets advisor is available 24/7 to assist with any special requests, ground transportation, catering, or itinerary changes.

From jet to journey, your experience is managed with precision, privacy, and professionalism.
