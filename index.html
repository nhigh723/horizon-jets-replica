    Frequently Asked Questions

    What type of jets can I book through Horizon Jets?

    Our fleet includes light, midsize, super midsize, and heavy jets from top manufacturers like Gulfstream, Bombardier, Cessna, and Embraer. You can browse and compare aircraft on our site or speak with an advisor.

    What is a Jet Card, and how does it work?

    A Jet Card is a prepaid program that gives you guaranteed hourly rates, access to a fleet, and priority booking. Horizon Jets Jet Cards are ideal for clients flying 25+ hours per year and offer fixed pricing, no repositioning fees, and exclusive perks.

    What are the benefits of flying private with Horizon Jets?

    • No TSA lines or delays

    • Access to 5,000+ airports

    • Tailored in-flight amenities

    • Discreet VIP service

    • Flexible Jet Card and on-demand options

    Can I bring pets on board?

    Absolutely. Many of our aircraft are pet-friendly. Just let us know in advance, and we’ll match you with an operator that accommodates pets comfortably and safely.
